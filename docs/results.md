# Results package

All values below are traceable to retained files under `results/` and were generated by **Simplified Python FANET Model — NOT NS-3**. Results are descriptive for the stated setup; they do not imply physical/radio realism, statistical significance, or universal superiority. Population SD is reported where the experiment runner emits it.

## B. Detection performance

Evaluation unit is a node. A node is predicted positive if any sufficiently observed window is `SUSPICIOUS` or `MALICIOUS`. Window counts are not substituted for node-level confusion counts.

| Condition | Runs | TP | TN | FP | FN | Pooled F1 | Recall / detection rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Normal | 20 | 0 | 200 | 0 | 0 | Undefined (no positive examples) | Undefined |
| p=.25 | 20 | 11 | 180 | 0 | 9 | .710 | .55 |
| p=.50 | 20 | 19 | 180 | 0 | 1 | .974 | .95 |
| p=.80 | 20 | 20 | 180 | 0 | 0 | 1.000 | 1.00 |

Normal FPR was 0/200 in this matrix. These detector thresholds were not tuned on this final matrix. Results do not establish broader detection performance. Seed-42 single-run confusion counts and every run's metrics are saved alongside the trace data.

## C. Routing comparison

Paired experiment: seeds 42–61, same normal/packet-drop scenario settings and traffic for both routing policies; 20 runs in each condition-policy cell.

| Scenario | Policy | PDR mean ± population SD | Loss mean | App throughput mean (Mbps) | Mean hops | Attacked-intermediate exposure | Route changes/run |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Normal | Baseline | .9503 ± .0300 | .0497 | .03892 | 1.648 | 0% | 385.85 |
| Normal | Trust-aware | .9503 ± .0300 | .0497 | .03892 | 1.665 | 0% | 387.50 |
| p=.80 | Baseline | .9167 ± .0446 | .0834 | .03755 | 1.648 | 4.43% | 385.85 |
| p=.80 | Trust-aware | .9353 ± .0404 | .0648 | .03831 | 1.670 | 1.94% | 388.00 |

Exposure is computed after route selection; the attacker identity does not enter the routing policy. Throughput is delivered application bytes per configured duration, not PHY throughput. This tested model showed a higher mean PDR and lower attacker exposure with trust-aware routing under p=.8, alongside slightly longer routes and more path changes. Do not generalize beyond the tested seeds/configuration. Delay and signaling overhead are **Not modeled/measured in the current simulator.**

Per-seed traces, saved configs, manifests, aggregates, and six figures are in `results/routing_comparison_seed42_61/figures/` and its sibling data files. Bars use per-seed means and population-SD error bars.

## D. ML held-out performance

The hardened metrics were recomputed into `results/ml_phase4_hardened/`; the previous `results/ml_phase4_200m/` output remains retained. Feature generation uses causal telemetry/trust snapshots. GroupShuffleSplit separates seed groups, not individual rows. Train seeds: 44–56, 58, 60, 61; held-out seeds: 42, 43, 57, 59. This yields 64,000 training and 16,000 test rows; all 16,000 test frames come from four held-out seeds and are correlated within run.

## E. XAI outputs

SHAP 0.54.1 ran against the trained Random Forest. Global mean absolute values were computed from an evenly spaced deterministic sample of 512 of the 16,000 held-out rows. The top global features were forwarding ratio (.1696), delivery ratio (.0937), packets dropped (.0877), and indirect trust (.0642). The held-out local example is seed 42, p=.25, UAV 7, t=76 s; it was positive in both ground truth and model prediction. Local signed contributions are in `results/xai_phase4_hardened/local_shap_explanation.csv`; global values are in `global_shap_importance.csv`. These explain model output, not causal impact. LIME was not implemented; SHAP was selected as the primary explainability method.

## F. Limitations and provenance

- The Python model is explicitly **NOT NS-3** and has no PHY/MAC, propagation, interference, retransmission, or per-hop delay model.
- No delay or control/signaling overhead values are emitted as measured metrics.
- Trust equations, thresholds, weights, and update rates are implementation choices, not prescribed/calibrated by the PDFs.
- Detection p=.25 misses 9/20 attacked-node runs; one-seed perfect results are demonstrations only.
- The routing study is a paired 20-seed descriptive experiment at one configured topology and attacker placement; no formal significance test was run.
- Only probabilistic packet-drop and deterministic transit blackhole are implemented and validated. Other YAML scenario files are not evidence of implemented attacks.
- LIME is not implemented.
- Streamlit is verified, but its installation in the shared Python environment conflicts with unrelated FastAPI's Starlette constraint. Use an isolated virtual environment.

See [limitations](limitations.md), [reproducibility](reproducibility.md), and the [final audit](final_audit.md) for full scope and environment notes.
